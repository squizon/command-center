✨ What is the Command Center?

The Command Center is your personal productivity dashboard designed for Executive Assistants and anyone who supports multiple leaders. It helps you:

  • Track tasks per leader with due dates and categories
  • Monitor what you're waiting on and when to follow up
  • Track tasks when covering for other EAs
  • Store files, notes, and preferences in one place
  • Keep a weekly diary and archive your accomplishments

📆 Today View

Your daily command post. When you open the app, this is what you see first.

What's shown:

  • Progress bar — Shows how many of today's tasks you've completed
  • Due today — Tasks due right now (with notes/context shown below)
  • Recurring tasks — Daily/weekly items that reset
  • Needs follow-up — Waiting items older than 2 days (nudges)
  • Reminders — Waiting items with a reminder set for today
  • Personal checklist — Your own daily to-do items

Actions in Today view:

  • ✓ button — Complete a task, resolve a nudge, or clear a reminder
  • → tmrw / → Mon — Push a task to tomorrow (or Monday if it's Friday)
💡 The Today view pulls from all your leader tabs automatically. Complete tasks here and they update everywhere.

👥 Leader Tabs

Each leader you support gets their own tab with a full task board.

Task columns:

  • 🔴 Overdue — Past due date
  • 🟡 This Week — Due this Mon–Fri
  • 🟢 Upcoming — Future tasks
  • 🔁 Recurring — Repeating tasks (daily, weekly, etc.)

Adding tasks:

Click + Add Task at the top of any leader tab. Set a title, optional due date, and type (one-time or recurring).

Quick actions:

  • Double-click a task title to edit it inline
  • Click a task to open the detail modal (add notes, change due date)
  • ✓ button marks it complete
  • × button deletes it

Other sections per leader:

  • ⏳ Waiting On — Things you're waiting to hear back on
  • 💌 Nudge Queue — Auto-generated follow-up drafts for items 2+ days old
  • 👤 Preferences — Store exec preferences (travel, food, contacts)
  • 📝 Notes — Freeform notes about this leader

⏳ Waiting On & Nudge Queue

Track everything you're waiting to hear back on.

Adding a waiting item:

  • Click + Add in the Waiting On section
  • Enter what you're waiting for, who from, and the source (Slack, Outlook, etc.)
  • Optionally set a reminder date

Age badges:

  • 🟢 Green — 0–2 days (fresh)
  • 🟡 Yellow — 3–5 days (getting stale)
  • 🔴 Red — 6+ days (needs attention)

Nudge Queue:

When a waiting item hits 2+ days, the app auto-generates a polite follow-up message you can copy and paste into Slack or email. Click 📋 Copy to grab it.

💡 Mark items as ✓ resolved when you get a response. They're logged in your monthly stats.

🌿 Coverage

Use the Coverage tab to track tasks when you're covering for a colleague who's out, and compile a coverage report for when they return.

How it works:

  • Click + Add Coverage Lane to create a new coverage period
  • Name it (e.g., "Covering for Maria — June 5–9")
  • Add tasks, notes, and handoff information
  • When coverage is done, click ✓ Complete to archive it to the Diary
  • Use × Remove to delete a lane without archiving (can't be undone)

📧 Handoff Email:

Click the 📧 Handoff button on any coverage lane to auto-generate an email summary. It opens Outlook with a pre-filled message listing completed tasks, open items, and your handoff notes — ready to send to whoever needs the update.

Coverage Notes:

Use the notes section to capture anything that comes up during coverage. You can move items to specific leader tabs later.

💡 The handoff email is a great way to document what happened while someone was out — no manual typing needed.

✦ Overview

Your bird's-eye view across all leaders.

What you'll find:

  • All Overdue — Every overdue task across all leaders
  • All Waiting On — Everything you're waiting on, combined
  • Ready to Nudge — Items that need follow-up
  • This Week's Stats — Open tasks, completed this week, waiting count
  • Weekly Summary — Editable text area for your end-of-week recap
  • Monthly Stats — Bigger picture numbers

Archive This Week:

Your weekly summary is automatically archived every Friday when you open the app. It saves to the Diary as a permanent record of what you accomplished that week. You can also click 📦 Archive This Week manually anytime if you want to save it early.

📖 Diary

Your historical record — everything archived lives here.

Sections:

  • Weekly Diary — Past weekly summaries (auto-archived every Friday)
  • Coverage History — Past coverage periods and what you handled
  • Past Events — Completed morale events
  • Past Months — Monthly workload stats
  • Other / Promo Material — Wins, shoutouts, accomplishments worth remembering
💡 The "Other" section is great for performance review prep. Add entries throughout the year so you never forget your wins.

📁 Files

Store screenshots, documents, and reference materials right in the app.

How to add files:

  • Upload — Click the Upload button to browse your computer

Folders:

Organize files into folders. Click + New Folder to create one. You can rename or delete folders anytime.

Image gallery:

Click any image to open it full-size. Use the arrow buttons to navigate between images in the same folder.

💡 Files are stored in your browser. Use Export in Settings to back them up.

⚙️ Settings

Make the Command Center yours.

Mascot:

  • Change the speech bubble text
  • Upload a custom mascot image (your pet, a character, anything)
  • Mute or change the hover sound
  • Upload a custom sound (MP3) — find a short bark, meow, or any clip you like, and upload it as your mascot's hover sound
  • Remove the mascot entirely if you prefer

How to upload a custom sound:

Go to Settings → Mascot → Sound section. Click the upload button and choose a short MP3 file from your computer (1–3 seconds works best). The sound will play whenever you hover over your mascot.

Theme:

  • Choose from preset light mode backgrounds (Lavender, Rose, Mint, Sunset, Gray, White)
  • Choose from preset dark mode backgrounds (Deep Blue, Purple Night, Forest, Warm Dark, Charcoal, Midnight)
  • Set a custom background color or gradient
  • Change the font family
  • Toggle dark mode with the 🌙 button (top-left corner)

Data:

  • Export — Save a backup file to your computer
  • Import — Restore from a backup file
  • Reset — Clear everything and start fresh

⌨️ Tips & Shortcuts

Clipboard Capture:

When you copy text from Slack or Outlook and switch back to the Command Center, it detects the clipboard content and offers to save it as a task, waiting item, or note. You can assign it to any leader instantly.

⚠️ Clipboard permission:

The first time you use the Command Center, your browser will ask: "See text and images copied to the clipboard — Allow or Block?"

You need to click Allow for the clipboard capture feature to work. If you accidentally clicked Block:

  • Click the 🔒 lock icon (or tune icon) in your browser's address bar
  • Find "Clipboard" in the permissions list
  • Change it from "Block" to "Allow"
  • Refresh the page

Search:

The search bar at the top searches across all tasks, notes, and waiting items. Click a result to jump to it.

Keyboard:

  • Esc — Close any open modal or image preview
  • ← → — Navigate images in the gallery
💡 Double-click any task title to edit it inline without opening the detail modal.

📱 Install as an App

You can install the Command Center as a standalone app on your computer — it'll show up in your taskbar and open in its own window, just like a regular program.

How to install:

  • In Chrome or Edge: Look for the install icon (⊕) in the address bar, or go to the browser menu (⋮) → "Install Command Center"
  • Click Install when prompted
  • It'll appear in your taskbar and Start menu
💡 Once installed, it opens in its own window without the browser toolbar — feels like a real desktop app.

💾 Data & Privacy

Your data is stored entirely in your browser's local storage. Here's what that means:

  • ✅ Private — Nothing is sent to any server
  • ✅ Fast — Everything loads instantly, no network needed
  • ⚠️ Browser-specific — Data lives in one browser only
  • ⚠️ Clearable — Clearing browser data removes your info

Good to know:

  • Deleting files on your computer does NOT affect your Command Center data — they're stored separately in the browser
  • If you switch browsers or devices, export your data first and import it in the new browser

Best practices:

  • Export a backup weekly (Settings → Export My Data)
  • Keep the backup file somewhere safe (cloud drive, USB, etc.)
💡 Set a recurring task for yourself: "Export Command Center backup" every Friday.
